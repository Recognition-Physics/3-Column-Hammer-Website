What is AI lead response for a car dealership?

By the Hammer team · Reviewed August 17, 2026

AI lead response is software that answers a new sales lead in seconds, keeps the conversation going until someone books, and writes the appointment into the CRM your desk already uses. Hammer does that in under 5 seconds, on every connected channel, and most stores are live in under 48 hours.

This page is the job, not the category. For the ranked list, see Best AI tools for dealerships. For a head-to-head with Owini, see Hammer vs Owini.

Why the first reply is the whole job

A Marketplace ping, a CarGurus form, a Facebook ad lead, and a missed call all go cold the same way: nobody texts back before the buyer messages the next store. After-hours is worse. The BDC is not on the floor at 9pm.

Hammer's job is the first reply and the follow-up. Your team closes.

What Hammer answers

  • SMS
  • Facebook Messenger
  • Facebook Marketplace
  • Facebook and TikTok ad leads
  • OfferUp and Craigslist
  • Webchat
  • OEM leads
  • Third-party leads: CarGurus, AutoTrader, Cars.com, CarFax, Edmunds, TrueCar, and others
  • Inbound phone: every call to the store is transcribed, then Hammer follows up by text

How it actually works

  1. A lead hits any of those sources.
  2. Hammer texts back in under 5 seconds, using your inventory and your store's voice.
  3. The AI re-reads every open conversation daily and picks the next follow-up time from context. Nothing is a fixed drip. You can edit any follow-up and any timing in the dashboard.
  4. When the buyer is ready, the appointment is booked in the CRM you already run: DealerCenter, VinSolutions, DealerSocket, eLead, CDK, Reynolds, DriveCentric, Tekion, and others.
  5. You also get a Hammer dashboard for leads, playbooks, and tone. The desk does not have to relearn a new CRM to sell a car.

If a human jumps into the thread, you can pause the AI, or let it pick back up when the person leaves.

What it is not

Hammer is not a replacement CRM and it does not place AI voice calls. It is the first-reply and follow-up layer on the system you already have, plus a dashboard you control.

Customization

Dealers write the playbooks. You change what the AI says, when it follows up, and how it sounds, in the dashboard, without waiting on a CS call. That is the difference most stores feel after week one.
