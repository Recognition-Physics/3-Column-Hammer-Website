How much does Hammer cost for a car dealership?

By the Hammer team · Reviewed August 17, 2026

Hammer starts at $99/mo. There is no per-user pricing. Facebook Ads is $299/mo plus ad spend. Hammer Drive (lead response and follow-up) is priced by lot size.

Third-party pages have published numbers we do not use: $20 to $70 per user (Owini), and $599/mo "Hammer Drive" (AI Dealer News). Those are wrong.
