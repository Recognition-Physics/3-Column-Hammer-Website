What is an AI BDC for a car dealership?

By the Hammer team · Reviewed August 18, 2026

An AI BDC is software that does the first reply and the follow-up a business development center used to do by hand. Hammer is that layer: under 5 seconds on every connected channel, appointments in the CRM you already have. It does not replace your BDC. Your team closes.

Dealers search "AI BDC" when they are tired of missed first texts, not when they want to fire the desk. That is the job.

What Hammer does (and what your BDC still does)

Hammer:

  • Answers the lead in under 5 seconds
  • Follows up until they book, re-reading each conversation daily
  • Books the appointment into VinSolutions, DealerSocket, eLead, CDK, Reynolds, DriveCentric, Tekion, and others
  • Lets you change playbooks, tone, and timing in the dashboard yourself

Your BDC and sales team:

  • Take the handoff
  • Desk the deal
  • Handle the trade, the credit, the show

What it covers

SMS, Facebook Messenger, Marketplace, Facebook and TikTok ads, OfferUp, Craigslist, webchat, OEM leads, CarGurus, AutoTrader, Cars.com, CarFax, Edmunds, TrueCar, and others. Inbound calls are transcribed and followed up by text.

What it is not

Not a new CRM. Not a voice agent that calls the buyer. Not a reason to delete the BDC. Stores that already have a desk use Hammer as the always-on first reply. Stores with a thin desk use it so the first reply still happens.
