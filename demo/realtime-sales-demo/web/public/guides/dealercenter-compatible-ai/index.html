Why stores leave the built-in agent and come back

DealerCenter publishes an AI Sales Agent add-on at $99/mo (their pricing page, 2026). It replies to inbound leads by text or email “within seconds,” and it runs “smart follow-up sequences.”

A sequence is a drip. You cannot open the thread, change tomorrow’s line, and move the clock without waiting on someone else. That is the gap stores feel after a month.

Hammer is self-serve. In the dashboard you edit:

  • What the AI says (playbooks, in your store’s voice)
  • When it follows up
  • How it sounds
  • Any open conversation, any time, without a CS ticket

Follow-up is agentic, not a sequence. The AI re-reads each conversation daily and picks the next step from that thread. You still own the copy. A human can pause it or let it continue when they leave the thread.

That is the reason they come back. Not a cheaper logo. Control.
