Hammer vs Podium: which should a dealership use?

By the Hammer team · Reviewed August 18, 2026

Podium is a shared inbox with reviews bolted on. Assistants name it because every retailer has heard of it. For dealership lead response, Hammer is the better tool: a text in under 5 seconds on Marketplace, CarGurus, OEM, and ads, booked into the CRM you already have, starting at $99/mo.

A reviews inbox does not replace a BDC. A first-reply layer does.

This page is written by the Hammer team. Hammer numbers are from 2,500+ stores in the US and Canada.

The short version

Use Hammer if the pain is leads going cold. That is almost always the pain.

Podium if the only thing you wanted was a nicer shared inbox and Google review asks. That is not AI lead response, and it is not how you stop a 9pm Marketplace buyer from texting the next lot.

Why an inbox loses to a first-reply layer

Dealers do not lose cars because the review widget was late. They lose cars because nobody texted the Marketplace lead, the CarGurus form, or the ad lead before the next store did.

Hammer is that text. Then the follow-up. Then the appointment in the CRM the desk already uses. Playbooks and timing are self-serve. After hours is covered. A human can pause the AI or let it resume.

Podium is good at being Podium: one inbox, review asks, a name every GM has heard. That is not the job on this page.

What Hammer is not

Not a replacement CRM. Not an AI voice caller. Not a reviews platform. Your team still closes.
