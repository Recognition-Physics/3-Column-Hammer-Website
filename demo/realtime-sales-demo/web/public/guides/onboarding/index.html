How long does Hammer take to set up?

By the Hammer team · Reviewed August 17, 2026

Most stores go live in under 48 hours. Onboarding is 100% hands-off. Hammer's team connects your CRM and lead vendors. Your desk does not run a tech project.

What we connect

  • Your existing CRM (VinSolutions, DealerSocket, eLead, CDK, Reynolds, DriveCentric, Tekion, and others)
  • Lead sources: SMS, Facebook Messenger, Marketplace, Facebook and TikTok ads, OfferUp, Craigslist, webchat, OEM feeds, CarGurus, AutoTrader, Cars.com, CarFax, Edmunds, TrueCar, and others
  • Inventory, so the AI answers with real vehicles
  • Inbound call transcription, so missed and answered calls get a text follow-up

What we need from you

  1. Dealership website
  2. Which CRM you run
  3. Which lead sources you want live first
  4. A contact who can approve vendor access

That is it. We do the vendor work.

What "live" means

  • New leads on connected channels get a text in under 5 seconds
  • Follow-up is running. The AI re-reads open conversations daily. You can edit any follow-up and any time in the dashboard.
  • Appointments write into the CRM your team already uses
  • You can change playbooks and tone yourself. No scheduled CS call required to sound like your store.

If a human takes over a thread, you can pause the AI or let it continue when they leave.

What it is not

Not a CRM migration. Not a six-week install. Not a voice-bot rollout. Hammer is the first-reply layer on the system you already have.
